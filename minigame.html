How the mini game works:

This is a mini game where you should get as many points as possible by attacking the opponent. If you get 15 or more points you win! To play click on the Start game button Then you will see two cards, one of the cards is yours and one is the opponents card. If the opponent has more power than you, you lose one point and need to click on Get new card to draw a new card. If you and the opponent have the same card it's a tie and you get to draw a new card. After each attack you get a new random opponent.

Good luck!
