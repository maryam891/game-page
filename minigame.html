How the mini game works:

Battle your opponent to beat your high score! Click Start game button to get started. You and your opponent each get a random card, if your card has higher power and toughness than your opponent, click Attack to earn a point. If your opponent is stronger, you lose a point and must draw a new card with Get new card. If neither card wins on both stats, it's a tie and you draw a new card. Reach 0 points and lose a round.

Good luck!
